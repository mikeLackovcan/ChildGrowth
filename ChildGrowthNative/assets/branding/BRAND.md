# Quest Champ brand

Quest Champ is a friendly habit-and-chore adventure for children. Everyday responsibilities become short quests, while the child’s chosen hero reacts, celebrates progress, and helps them know what to do next.

## Core palette

- Champion blue: `#2563EB`
- Friendly sky: `#38BDF8`
- Quest lime: `#B8F23D`
- Reward gold: `#F9B233`
- Warm coral: `#FF6B6B`
- Ink navy: `#132447`

## Store positioning

Short description: **Habits and chores become fun daily quests for kids.**

Long description: **Quest Champ helps children build routines through simple visual quests. Kids complete chores, homework, focus sessions, and healthy habits; their chosen hero celebrates progress with coins, XP, character items, and family rewards. Parents can create quests, choose reminders, review progress, and link securely with a child account.**
