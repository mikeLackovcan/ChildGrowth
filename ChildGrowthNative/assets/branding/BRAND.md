# QuestBlox brand

QuestBlox is a cheerful family quest tracker: everyday responsibilities become small adventures with XP, coins, prizes, and parent-approved progress.

## Core palette

- Cobalt: `#1264E8`
- Sky: `#35C2F4`
- Treasure gold: `#F5A900`
- Star yellow: `#FFE15A`
- Coral accent: `#FF6B5E`
- Ink navy: `#0B2455`

## Store positioning

Short description: **Turn everyday tasks into exciting quests and rewards.**

Long description: **QuestBlox helps families turn routines into adventures. Parents create quests, approve progress, and choose real-world rewards. Kids complete daily challenges, earn XP and coins, unlock avatars, and build momentum one quest at a time.**
